Anchor Bolt Introduction

What is an anchor bolt?

An anchor bolt is a steel fastener used to connect a structure, equipment or steel component to a concrete foundation.

One portion of the bolt is embedded in concrete while the exposed portion is used with a nut and washer to secure the structure. The final configuration depends on the load, installation and project requirement.

Explore technical basics →

How an Anchor Bolt Works

Simple connection. Critical role.

  1. 01Bolt is positioned in the foundation
  2. 02Concrete is poured around the embedded portion
  3. 03Concrete hardens around the anchor
  4. 04Base plate is positioned and secured
  5. 05Nut and washer clamp the structure

Technical Knowledge

Useful anchor bolt basics.

Clear terms make it easier to communicate a project requirement.

What does M16 mean?

Metric thread with a nominal diameter of approximately 16 mm.

What is thread pitch?

The distance between corresponding points on adjacent threads.

What is embedment?

The portion of an anchor installed inside the concrete.

What is bend radius?

The radius created at the bend of an L/J anchor bolt.

Why is material grade important?

Material grade determines mechanical properties such as strength and ductility.
